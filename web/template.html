Time and mechanical loading by activity

Loading figures are mechanical proxies computed from acceleration at the sensor (accumulated acceleration change, peak acceleration, rhythm). They are not joint forces or moments. One rhythm cycle is about two steps when walking. The index (0 to 100) is the percentile of the loading rate among about 13,000 reference windows from 16 people.

Model card

Limits. Trained on MotionSense: 24 adults, iPhone in the front trouser pocket, 50 Hz, six activities (walking, jogging, up and down stairs, sitting, standing). Other sensor placements, other activities (for example squats, cycling, driving) and very different gait (injury, children, older adults) will be misclassified or flagged as low confidence. The gravity direction is part of the input, so mounting orientation matters.

Model and data processing: see the project README. Dataset: Malekzadeh et al., MotionSense (MIT licence). Your file is not uploaded anywhere.
